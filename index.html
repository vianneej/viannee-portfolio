0000 / A little about me

Making the internet a little more human.

I'm Viannee, a Computer Engineering student at the University of Florida who enjoys moving between thoughtful design and hands-on engineering. My work spans UI/UX, full-stack web applications, and embedded systems, with a focus on making technical ideas feel clear, useful, and welcoming.

Through my work with SHPE, I have helped create accessible experiences while building the technical foundation behind them. I also love working close to the hardware: designing ESP32 rovers, experimenting with Arduino-powered projects, and learning through C++, VHDL, and AVR Assembly. My AI Fundamentals + Applications certificate reflects the same curiosity I bring to every project: understand how the system works, then find a more human way to use it.
